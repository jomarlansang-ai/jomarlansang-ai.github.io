Questions

What is Crazy Dragon Control Center?

Crazy Dragon Control Center is an unofficial companion for MIR4 players on PlayTechCorner. It has today's bosses, a 53-boss schedule, a daily checklist, character compare, an expedition calculator, and a clan board.

Is Crazy Dragon Control Center affiliated with Wemade?

No. This is an unofficial fan site, not affiliated with Wemade. MIR4 is a trademark of Wemade.

What does Crazy Dragon Control Center store?

Favorites, checklist ticks, clan records, expedition runs, planner notes, and comparison entries stay in this browser. You can export or import that data as JSON. The site does not take accounts.

Do the boss timers track live spawns?

No. Timers are schedule estimates from a table checked 4 Oct 2026. Boss presence is not tracked. A row is marked spawning soon 30 minutes before the scheduled time, and the live window is 10 minutes, or 5 minutes for Purgatory Quest Bosses.

How do I send feedback?

Open Feedback in Crazy Dragon Control Center. It emails playtechcorner@gmail.com unless a form endpoint is configured.
