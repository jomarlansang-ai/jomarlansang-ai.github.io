Questions

What is MIR4 Helper?

MIR4 Helper is an unofficial day-to-day section of PlayTechCorner. It has a daily checklist, an event and boss clock in Philippine Time, a weekly planner, a hand-written digest of official notice links, and a clan board that links to the Crazy Dragon app.

Is MIR4 Helper affiliated with Wemade?

No. This is an unofficial fan site, not affiliated with Wemade. MIR4 is a trademark of Wemade.

What does MIR4 Helper store?

Ticks, planner notes, and clan notes stay in this browser localStorage. The site does not take accounts, and it does not scrape the game.

How do I send feedback?

Email playtechcorner@gmail.com. There is no third-party form.
