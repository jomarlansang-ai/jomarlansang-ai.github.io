Crazy Dragon clan board

Unofficial fan site, not affiliated with Wemade. MIR4 is a trademark of Wemade.

Crazy Dragon is a fan-made MIR4 clan management tool for clan leaders and members. It records donations and gold contributions, organizes member lists, and tracks expedition participation. Reports can be copied into Discord or Messenger. Members do not need to log in.

Developer: PlayTechCorner. Google Play listing updated 2 February 2026. Free, contains ads, rated Everyone. The web app keeps data in the browser and does not sync with the phone app.

This page does not publish a member list, donation totals, or other clan stats.
