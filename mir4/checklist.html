Questions

When does the MIR4 daily checklist reset?

It resets at 00:00 Philippine Time. That is the verified Magic Square and Secret Peak free-entry reset, which the official portal guide lists as 16:00 UTC.

Do all MIR4 dailies reset at 17:00 PHT?

That claim is not confirmed. The research brief could not verify a wiki page that said every daily resets at 17:00 ASIA, so this checklist does not use 17:00.

Where are checklist ticks saved?

In this browser only, under localStorage. They clear when the Philippine Time date changes at 00:00. They are not sent to PlayTechCorner.

Which checklist tasks are official?

The fifteen daily categories come from the official task guide, updated August 20, 2026. Clan donate, shop summons, event check-in, and vigor are common player habits, not extra official categories.
