Questions

What does the weekly planner save?

Your day notes and the boxes you tick, for the current week, in this browser localStorage.

When does a planner week start?

Monday at 00:00 PHT, the same moment some clan shop tickets reset. A new week starts with empty notes and ticks.

Does the planner know the next Castle Siege date?

No. Siege is about every four weeks on Sunday, and a September 27, 2026 notice is only an example that has passed. The next date is to confirm. You can tick the Castle Siege week box if you already know.
