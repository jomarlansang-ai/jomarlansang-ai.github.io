Questions

Which time zone does the MIR4 clock use?

Philippine Time (PHT, UTC+8). That is the same offset as the ASIA regional server time in the official notices cited here. Other regions are not listed.

Which MIR4 times are verified?

Magic Square and Secret Peak free entries reset at 00:00 PHT. Domination runs 09:00–13:00, 15:00–19:00, and 21:00–01:00 PHT. Nerkan is Tuesday at 19:00 sealed and 23:00 fight. Turkan is Thursday at 19:00 sealed and 23:00 fight. Some clan shop tickets reset Monday at 00:00 PHT. The Boosting World IV maintenance date is January 5, 2027. The hour of that maintenance is to confirm.

What does to confirm mean on the clock?

The research brief did not verify that time for the current game. The page keeps the entry so the gap is visible, and it does not present the time as official.

How do I report a wrong MIR4 time?

Use the Report a wrong time link on this page. It emails playtechcorner@gmail.com.
