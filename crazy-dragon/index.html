Crazy Dragon

  • Free
  • Contains ads
  • Rated Everyone

Crazy Dragon is a fan-made MIR4 clan management tool for clan leaders and members. It records donations and gold contributions, organizes member lists, and tracks expedition participation. Reports can be copied into Discord or Messenger. Members do not need to log in.

Developer: PlayTechCorner. Google Play listing updated 2 February 2026.

Crazy Dragon is a fan-made utility app for MIR4 players. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Wemade or MIR4’s official developers. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Features

  • Record which members donated today. The list shows who has and has not donated, and you can copy a report for Discord or Messenger.

  • Track gold contributions separately from the daily donation list, and copy a gold donation report.

  • Organize the member list by name, class, and role. Search and filter the list. Scanning members from a photo is available in the Android app.

  • Record which expedition bosses the clan will run and compare the cost with clan resources in the expedition calculator.

  • Copy donation and expedition summaries for Discord, Messenger, or group chats.

  • Clan members do not create accounts. Records stay on the device, or in this browser for the web app.

  • A simple clan tracker without a member registration system.
