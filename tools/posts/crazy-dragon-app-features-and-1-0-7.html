Crazy Dragon is an unofficial companion app for MIR4 players from PlayTechCorner. It is free on Google Play and contains ads. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Wemade.

Features

  • Daily donation tracker: see who has and has not donated today, and copy a report for Discord or Messenger.
  • Gold donation tracker, kept separate from the daily list.
  • Clan member management by name, class and role, with search and filters. Scanning members from a photo is available in the Android app.
  • Expedition tracking with an expedition calculator.
  • No member login: records stay on the device, or in the browser for the web app.

Version 1.0.7

The Crazy Dragon page describes features coming in version 1.0.7, including a “Today” view and a daily checklist. Version 1.0.7 is not confirmed live on Google Play. Release date: to confirm.
