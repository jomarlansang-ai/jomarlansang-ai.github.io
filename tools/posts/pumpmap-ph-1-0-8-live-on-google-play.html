PumpMap PH version 1.0.8 is now publicly available on Google Play. It was published on Oct 26, 2026. The app is free and has no ads.

What PumpMap PH does

  • Google-confirmed gas stations shown on a map.
  • Fuel price pins so you can see prices on the map.
  • Offline price updates: you can update a price without signal and it syncs later.
  • Report missing stations from the app.

New in 1.0.8

From the PumpMap PH page: a first-launch tour that explains each button, an EV filter next to Diesel, an “Add station” tab for reporting a missing gas station or EV charger (an admin approves each report), map pins that group together when you zoom out, and a fix for a false “Offline” banner.

What's next

The PumpMap PH page lists weekly DOE fuel prices (planned, v2) and richer EV charger details (planned, v3). Release dates: to confirm.
