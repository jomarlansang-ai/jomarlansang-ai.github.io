Privacy policy

PumpMap PH is an Android app from PlayTechCorner.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The app

The app is free. It has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Developer: PlayTechCorner

Device location

Device location is used to show nearby stations and to allow a fuel-price report only when the phone is within 250 meters.

Google sign-in

Google sign-in is optional. It is required only to submit a price report or a station report.

Price reports and station reports

Price reports and station reports are sent to https://pumpmap-live-stations-574067858185.asia-southeast1.run.app.

Saved stations

Saved stations stay on the phone.
