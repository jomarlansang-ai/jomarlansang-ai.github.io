Gas stations, EV chargers, and fuel prices across the Philippines, even offline.

Free for Android. Privacy

Now live on Google Play

Google approved PumpMap PH for production on Oct 26, 2026. Version 1.0.8 is live in the Philippines. It's free, and it shows nearby gas stations and EV chargers on a map, with fuel prices reported by drivers.

New in 1.0.8

  • A first-launch tour that explains each button.
  • An EV filter next to Diesel.
  • An “Add station” tab for reporting a missing gas station or EV charger. An admin approves each report.
  • Cleaner map pins that group together when you zoom out.
  • A fix for a false “Offline” banner.
  • A “Sponsor or advertise with PumpMap” contact in Profile: playtechcorner@gmail.com.

What it does

  • Shows nearby gas stations and EV chargers on a map.
  • Shows fuel prices reported by drivers.
  • Filters for EV chargers, next to Diesel.
  • Lets you report a missing gas station or EV charger from the “Add station” tab. An admin approves each report.
  • Groups map pins together when you zoom out.
  • Has a first-launch tour that explains each button.
