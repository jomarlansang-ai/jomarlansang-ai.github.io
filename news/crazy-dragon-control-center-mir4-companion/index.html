Crazy Dragon Control Center: an unofficial MIR4 companion in your browser

By PlayTechCorner · Oct 28, 2026

Unofficial fan content. Not affiliated with Wemade. MIR4 is a trademark of Wemade.

Crazy Dragon Control Center is an unofficial companion for MIR4 players on PlayTechCorner. It runs in the browser and does not take accounts.

What's inside

  • Today's bosses and a 53-boss schedule.
  • A daily checklist.
  • Character compare and an expedition calculator.
  • A clan board.

About the timers

Boss timers are schedule estimates from a table checked 4 Oct 2026; boss presence is not tracked. A row is marked “spawning soon” 30 minutes before the scheduled time, and the live window is 10 minutes (5 minutes for Purgatory Quest Bosses).

Your data

Favorites, checklist ticks, clan records, expedition runs, planner notes and comparison entries stay in your browser. You can export or import them as JSON.
